## Recognition
- **Salt Lake Tribune (Sept 30, 2024):** makes doughnuts "the best in Salt Lake County and, some say, in Utah."
- **Mashed** (reported by iHeart, Mar 2022): best donut shop in Utah; recommends arriving early; known for bear claws (apple-filled).
- Rated 4.8 on aggregated reviews; reviewers say there's "consistently a line" and that it "often sells out." Visiting around 2 PM can mean slim pickings. **This is our sold-out problem, in customers' own words.**
